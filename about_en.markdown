## About Me
[한국어로 보기](/about_kr.markdown)

Hello! I’m a **Data Engineer** with a penchant for exploring the intersection of technology and real-world applications. My journey into tech began during my high school days, where my first project—building an air quality tester with **Arduino** and lasers—sparked my interest in the digital realm. I pursued this passion through my studies in Engineering Physics at the **University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign**.

Today, I work extensively with **Python** to ensure the integrity and quality of data in our systems. My technical expertise spans a range of tools and languages, including **JavaScript**, **Rust**, and **C++**, but Python is where I feel most at home. I’m also proficient in several languages—**Korean**, **English**, **Mandarin Chinese**, and **Spanish**—which helps me collaborate across diverse teams and environments.

As a certified GIS analyst with credentials in **ArcGIS Pro for Professionals**, I’ve developed a unique skill set that marries geospatial data with robust data engineering principles. I’m continually expanding my knowledge, currently diving deeper into cloud platforms like **AWS** and **GCP** to enhance my skills in web development and database management.

The creation of this blog is a step towards documenting my learning journey. It serves as a personal repository where I can consolidate and reflect on my experiences—ensuring I truly own the knowledge I acquire. While you’ll find a focus on data engineering, web development, and GIS here, I’ll also delve into any topic that captures my curiosity.

Join me as I navigate through the evolving landscape of tech, sharing insights and lessons along the way.
